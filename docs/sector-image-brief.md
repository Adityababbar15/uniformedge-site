## The one hard rule

**No readable text, logos, badges or name tags on any garment.** Generators produce
garbled lettering, and a fake-looking logo on a uniform is worse than none. Leave the
chest and sleeve areas clean — real branding is composited later.

```
watermark, signature
```

## Check before sending them over

Generators fail most often on hands, and every one of these has visible hands.
Look at: the clipboard grip (S-02, S-04), the plating hands (S-03), and anyone
gesturing mid-conversation (S-01). Regenerate rather than accept a bad hand — it is
the single thing that makes an image read as fake.
